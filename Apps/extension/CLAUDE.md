# Browser extension (`Apps/extension/`)

The rules for the whole repo are in the root CLAUDE.md.

- Browser extension (`Apps/extension/`, Chrome/Edge MV3, minimum Chrome 127 (`chrome.action.openPopup`), no framework; its icons are drawn by the build from `Apps/extension/logo.svg`, the «ذكرى» logo as vector paths, on a light tile), laid out after Quran Tab: the new tab (`newtab.ts` + `newtab/*`): four looks (light, dark, and frosted glass light/dark over a photo: `Apps/extension/backgrounds.json`, Wikimedia Commons featured/quality images with author and license shown in the settings, picked by hand; favourites (automatic change then rotates among them), a blur amount, or a photo from the device; the next tab's photo is chosen and preloaded ahead and `boot.ts` paints it before load), the clock and dates, «أذكار وأدعية» as one button, a shortcuts dock on any edge in three sizes (links and groups of links, ready-made groups, each with its site's favicon via the `favicon` permission or a chosen picture kept in local storage; an editor window with drag in/out of groups; or the optional `topSites`), the ayah card (a curated passage or the mushaf in order, drawn with the QCF V2 page fonts kept in Cache Storage by `lib/fonts.ts`, the next tab's passage and fonts prepared ahead; ayat by global index 0..6235 in `lib/quran.ts`; recitation with volume, «تكرار مقطع» over the ayat/page/surah/juz/a range, favourites, التفسير الميسر under it or in a window, and «نسخ ومشاركة» as text or an image drawn on a canvas in the site's image looks and post sizes, `newtab/share.ts`) and the prayer card (timeline, names on hover when compact, mute; city, method, Asr madhab (`Place.madhab`), minute adjustments and notices in the settings). The adhan (full, short or none; 11 muezzins in `Apps/extension/adhan`, taken from Quran Tab's set at the owner's request; volume) plays from the service worker through the offscreen document, which also plays the toolbar popup's player (turned down during the adhan): live stations, recorded recitations from mp3quran.net (`data/reciters.json` embedded as `__RECITERS__`, `lib/reciters.ts`; a surah goes on to the next and is resumed where it was left, `radio.resume`), and the listening wird (a juz a day ayah by ayah from everyayah.com with the basmala before each surah, progress in `wird`); `lib/radio.ts` `Track` is what plays, the offscreen document plays a live stream or a list of recordings and reports where it is (`radio.pos`, `radio.list` says what each item is); the popup has favourites and the last played (`Settings.listen.stations/reciters`, `radio.recent`), surahs suggested for the time (`lib/listen.ts` `suggestions`: Al-Kahf on Friday, Al-Mulk at night, Al-Baqarah every few days, with the reciter `listen.moshaf`), a sleep timer (minutes, or the end of the surah: the next live title or `stop-after`, faded out) and a gear to the «الاستماع» settings page (`#settings-listen`); the service worker also rings a Quran alarm (before fajr or at a set time, rising over a minute) and a Friday 10:00 Al-Kahf notice with «استمع»; the worker sends prayer/azkar notifications with `chrome.alarms` and shows the time to the next prayer on the icon. Also: a tools corner (each can be hidden): weather from Open-Meteo (`newtab/weather.ts`, 7 days, cached 30 min), to-do lists with reminders the service worker notifies (`newtab/todo.ts`, «تم» from the notification), notes (`newtab/notes.ts`), and the browser's bookmarks, history, extensions (enable/disable/remove) and installed apps in side panels (`newtab/browser.ts`, each optional permission asked on first click); a search box: in the Chrome build it only calls `chrome.search.query` (the browser's own default engine, `newtab/search.ts`: the Chrome Web Store rejected 1.5.0 for an engine picker and Google suggestions on the new tab, «Red Argon», single purpose — never put them back in Chrome's build, not even the engine list), in the Edge and Firefox builds an engine picker with optional Google suggestions (`newtab/search.full.ts`, `ENGINES`, optional host permission); ayah buttons placed by the settings (row / «المزيد» / hidden, `Settings.controls`); favourite ayat in a window by surah; ready-made link groups previewed with their logos (Google's favicon service, `favicon()` in dock.ts), «الأكثر زيارة» as its own group. Settings, to-dos (`todo.lists`) and notes (`notes`) live in `chrome.storage.local` (settings moved from sync once); with «المزامنة» on, the service worker copies them gzipped into `chrome.storage.sync` chunks (`lib/sync.ts`, ~95 KB max, tagged with this profile's `device.id`) half a minute after a change, and on browser start merges a newer copy from another computer (notes by last edit, to-dos by list/task id); files export/import the parts the user picks (settings, shortcuts with their pictures, to-dos, notes), merged or replacing. Also: the settings window is a sidebar of pages (`PAGES` in settings-dialog.ts); a zikr reminder every 15/30/60/120 min (`Settings.zikr`, the service worker's `zikr` alarm, a notification and, when chosen, the recording from `Apps/extension/zikr` played by the offscreen document; the phrases' words are cut out of hisn.json at build time, `zikrPhrases()`); the place by typed coordinates or by an address in Arabic or Latin letters (`lib/geocode.ts`: OpenStreetMap Nominatim on Enter only, as its policy asks, biased to the current place, Open-Meteo place names as a fallback; the time zone from Open-Meteo), which the weather follows too; shortcut icons from Chrome's own favicon when it has a real one else Google's faviconV2 by the full URL (`lib/icons.ts`, cached a week, «تحديث الأيقونات»); shortcuts can stay out of the bar (`hidden`), groups show as folders or laid out in «كل الاختصارات», installed apps as a group (`management`). Never the browser's confirm()/prompt(): `confirmDialog`/`promptDialog`/`choiceDialog` in lib/dom.ts. Optional permissions are always requested from the click (granted ones answer at once; `chrome.management` exists without its permission); a page open when one is granted gets its functions only after a reload, so `allowed()` reloads to a hash that reopens the panel (`#bookmarks`, `#history`, `#extensions`, `#apps`) and the dock reloads itself; «حدّد موقعي» asks for the optional `geolocation`. Local builds carry source maps (`--zip` builds don't). It imports the client-safe `src/lib` modules; `bun run ext:build` (esbuild, `scripts/build-extension.ts`) writes `Apps/extension/dist` (gitignored), `bun run ext:build -- --browser=edge|firefox` builds `dist-edge`/`dist-firefox`, `bun run ext:zip` all three and the store packages (`zekra-<version>-{chrome,edge,firefox}.zip`; no source maps; the builds differ by `__BROWSER__` and by files `browserVariants` swaps — `search.full.ts`, `lib/audio-host.firefox.ts` — and `manifestFor` writes each manifest: Firefox has a background page that plays the audio itself (`lib/audio-engine.ts`, shared with the offscreen document), its add-on id `zekra@zekra.site` and data collection `locationInfo`, no offscreen/favicon/management, notifications without buttons (`showNote`; a click does the button's job), no daily count; `bunx web-ext lint --source-dir Apps/extension/dist-firefox` must stay clean, and AMO wants the source zip and build steps in `store/listing.md`; the manifest description must stay ≤132 characters for the Chrome store; `bun run ext:store` draws the store icon and promo tiles and `bun run ext:shots` takes the 1280×800 screenshots from the built extension in Chrome (into `Apps/extension/store`, with `listing.md`, the store text)); once a UTC day the service worker sends `GET /api/ext/ping` (version, browser, which features are on; no id; opt-out `Settings.stats`), counted with the Cloudflare country in `usage_counts` under `ext:` keys (`src/lib/ext-stats.ts`), and the browser opens `/bye?v=` after an uninstall (`setUninstallURL`; counted once per visit, a reason from `src/lib/ext-reasons.ts`, a written note goes to the feedback inbox); both show on /dashboard/stats for super admins; the extension has no host permission (the ping is `no-cors`; `/api/radio/[id]` answers with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`), so keep any site endpoint it reads CORS-open instead of adding one; its privacy policy and terms (Arabic and English, for the stores) are `/extension/privacy` — update it with any new data flow; icons sent to Google's favicon service carry the site only (`siteOnly` in lib/icons.ts), bookmarks/history use the browser's `_favicon`; imported or synced to-dos/notes go through `cleanLists`/`cleanNotes` (lib/data.ts); `EXT_SITE_URL` overrides https://zekra.site.
